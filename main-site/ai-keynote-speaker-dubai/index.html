Keynote topics

AI strategy talks for decisions that matter.

Choose a starting point for your conference, CEO forum or leadership event. Each topic connects the possibilities of AI with the choices your audience faces.

01

AI strategy: from experimentation to direction

Where should AI create value in your organisation?

A leadership perspective on choosing priorities, challenging assumptions and connecting AI initiatives to business objectives. Explore how to assess an opportunity before committing people, budget and trust.

Audience takeawayA clearer set of questions for prioritising AI opportunities and defining what a useful first step should achieve.

02

Digital Employees: designing work around outcomes

What changes when AI can act, not just answer?

Explore AI systems that carry out defined work across a process. The conversation covers roles, context, access and escalation — and how people can direct Digital Employees towards a business objective.

Audience takeawayA practical way to frame a potential Digital Employee role, with clear boundaries and a named human owner.

03

Delegating to AI without surrendering judgement

Which decisions must remain yours?

When AI recommends or acts, leaders still need to set direction, question the result and take responsibility. Explore the difference between delegating execution and giving up accountability.

Audience takeawayA shared language for deciding what AI may do, what requires approval and when a person must intervene.

Planning your event

A few useful answers.

Is this suitable for CEOs without a technical background?

Yes. The focus is on strategy, priorities and leadership decisions. Technical ideas are explained through their business implications, so CEOs and cross-functional leadership teams can participate in the same conversation.

Can the keynote reflect our Dubai event and audience?

The event briefing is the place to agree the theme, audience profile, sector context and desired takeaway. Share the purpose of your conference or leadership gathering so we can discuss the most relevant topic and format.

What are Digital Employees?

Digital Employees are AI systems designed to carry out a defined role or workflow, using relevant context and tools within agreed limits. The keynote explores how to set objectives, assign a human owner and decide when the system must ask for help or approval.

Does the session cover human responsibility when delegating to AI?

Yes. Human judgement and accountability are central themes. The discussion examines which tasks can be delegated, which decisions need human approval and how leaders retain oversight when AI systems act.

How do we check availability and request a fee?

Book a conversation to discuss your event. Bring the date, location, audience, preferred format and any recording requirements. Availability, scope and fees are agreed directly for the engagement.
