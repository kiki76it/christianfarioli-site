leadership decision.

A practical, non-technical book for CEOs, founders and decision-makers who want to connect AI with the work that matters.

Meet Christian and Alessio for a thought-provoking conversation about the ideas behind The Quiet AI Advantage — and what they mean for your business.

01

From experimentation to business value

Why more AI tools do not automatically create more value, and how to identify the organisational friction AI should remove.

02

A workforce that brings both together

How AI Employees and human teams can form a high-performing hybrid workforce, with clear responsibility and human judgement.

03

The next 90 days of leadership

Moving towards governance, accountability and measurable ROI — and choosing the practical AI decisions to make next.
