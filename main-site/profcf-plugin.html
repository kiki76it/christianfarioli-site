About the plugin

Prof.CF (All Digital Marketing) is an AI assistant published by Prof. Christian Farioli for marketers, managers and business owners. It provides detailed explanations, practical examples and guidance with a warm, approachable Italian tone.

Topics include marketing strategy, analytics, social media, Google Ads, SEO, content, public relations, video, lead generation, landing pages, copywriting, storytelling, funnels, branding and productivity.

English is the default language; you can ask for another language.

Try asking

  • Build a 90-day digital marketing strategy for a small B2B training company.
  • Explain how AI can improve marketing analysis, using an illustrative example.
  • Help me improve the structure and copy of a landing page.

The plugin provides guidance using instructions and reference materials. It does not connect to advertising accounts, retrieve live campaign data, launch campaigns or process payments. Responses are AI-generated and are not personal messages from Prof. Christian Farioli.

Plugin privacy notice

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This notice explains how Prof. Christian Farioli handles information in connection with the Prof.CF (All Digital Marketing) plugin. It distinguishes use inside ChatGPT from visiting this website or contacting us separately.

1. Using the plugin inside ChatGPT

The plugin consists of instructions and reference materials. It has no publisher-operated backend, connected external account, analytics endpoint or payment system that receives your conversations. The publisher does not receive or retain your prompts, uploaded files or conversation history through the plugin.

ChatGPT processes your interactions under the OpenAI terms, privacy policy and account settings applicable to you. The absence of a publisher backend does not mean OpenAI does not process or retain data. See OpenAI’s privacy policy and the controls available in your ChatGPT account.

2. Contacting us separately

If you email info@christianfarioli.com, we receive your email address, any name you provide, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request, investigate the issue or handle your privacy enquiry. Our email service provider processes these communications to deliver and store them.

Separate support communications are handled under our website privacy policy, including its provisions on service providers, security, international processing and your rights.

3. Retention and deletion

Because the publisher does not receive conversations through the plugin, there is no separate publisher-held conversation archive to delete. OpenAI’s retention and deletion rules apply to data held within ChatGPT.

For information you send to us separately, we follow the retention approach in our website privacy policy: keeping it only for as long as needed for the relevant purpose or required by law. Email us to request access, correction or deletion of information held by the publisher. Requests concerning your ChatGPT account or information held by OpenAI should be directed to OpenAI.

4. Website visits and other services

Visiting christianfarioli.com is separate from using the plugin. Website hosting, security, cookies, analytics and any services you choose to use on the website are covered by our website privacy policy. The plugin itself does not add an external analytics connection or transmit your conversations to this website.

5. Questions and updates

For privacy questions about this plugin, email info@christianfarioli.com. If the plugin’s data practices change, this notice will be updated with a revised date.
